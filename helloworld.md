## Why am I doing this? 

I'm a words person, not so much a design and layout person. The part of maintaining a website and a blog which I find the most time consuming and least satisfying has to be managing everything to do with appearance. Periodically I will write for myself and then never put it anywhere because going through the process of getting it 'just so' takes the joy out of it. And let's be blunt, when very few people are going to actually read it the effort often feels wasted.

Yet if the design of the text can shift with the reader then all I have to do is be myself, and focus on the typos. I can lean into the markdown features I tend to use privately and it'll render accordingly. In a sense the reason I am dipping my toe in the water here is because it could well be the spark that I need to not only write more but share more often as well.
